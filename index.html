              Tanks (OpenStreetMap) · Mission Kakatiya

              village tanks, lakes and reservoirs mapped in OpenStreetMap: dots sized by water spread, the real outlines when zoomed in. “Restored 2015–18” shades each district by the tanks taken up under Mission Kakatiya. No source publishes which tanks were restored with their locations, so the before / after 2014 view is per district, not per tank.

              Groundwater · depth to water in May (India-WRIS)

              Monitoring wells of the Central Ground Water Board and the state Ground Water Department, each averaged over its May readings of 2010–14 and of 2015–23; districts take the mean of their wells. Depth: near the surface · 40 m down. Change: water table rose · fell. Click a well for its year-by-year readings.

              Proposal · river water for 85 % of farmland

              A planning sketch computed from this map's terrain, river-flow and land-cover data, not a design. Everything drawn is new and marked “new”: reservoir with its storage in TMC, lift delivery (balancing tank) with TMC a year, pump house with MW and lift, canal and tunnel with km and TMC a year, land that gravity can serve from them; new and existing stages of the six multi-stage trunk systems. Each work takes water from the cheapest source with spare flow: a river intake, a reservoir or canal an existing scheme already fills, or a reservoir proposed here, so lifts cascade in stages as Kaleshwaram's do. Opens the Proposal card.
