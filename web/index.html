Rivers & lifts

Rivers run east to the Bay of Bengal while much of the farmland sits higher. Lift schemes pump river water uphill in stages: Kaleshwaram climbs from Medigadda on the Godavari through barrages, pump houses, tunnels and reservoirs to Konda Pochamma Sagar.                 About & sources

                How to read the map

                Five lenses tell one story each: the heights of the plateau, the rivers and the lifts that pump water up it, what was built since Telangana was formed in 2014, groundwater and cropland before and after, and a computed proposal. Each lens sets its own layers; Details opens the tables and charts behind it.

                Scene. Map is Mapbox Standard (roads, towns, water, buildings in 3D when zoomed in; lit for day or night with the theme). Satellite is Mapbox Standard Satellite imagery; switching to it turns the relief colours off so the imagery shows. Night lights the same map for night.

                Tap readout. Tap or click any spot for its height above sea level, read from the Mapbox Terrain-DEM the map is drawing, with a landmark for scale. A click on a dam, pump house, link, canal, tank, well or proposed work opens that feature instead.

                Navigation. Right-drag or Ctrl-drag rotates and tilts, scroll zooms, two fingers rotate on touch. 3D switches between the tilted view and straight down, Orbit turns the view slowly, Reset returns to the lens's own view. The URL carries the view, so it can be shared.

                Height

                City and town heights are the median of the Copernicus DEM (ESA) around each place; the tap readout and the relief come from Mapbox Terrain-DEM, so the two can differ by a few metres. Heights are terrain models, not surveyed benchmarks.

                The relief is exaggerated: the slider value applies at district level, the plateau is raised more than twice as much at state level so it stands out from afar, and never less than the slider value up close. Elevation bands step the colours every 100 m so differences can be counted; contours carry their height every 200 m once zoomed in.

                Rivers & lifts

                Rivers. River lines show the long-term average flow of every stream from the HydroRIVERS model (HydroSHEDS, Lehner & Grill 2013): wider and darker means more water collected upstream, and bubbles ride the bigger rivers, denser and bigger with flow. The river line takes the relief colour of its bed.

                Dams and barrages. Labels show gross capacity; heights, years and inflow come from Wikipedia and HydroRIVERS. A dashed outline with a cone marks a work under construction or proposed; a dotted ring means position approximate (the village, not the structure).

                Links. Link lines are schematic straight lines between the structures, not the real canal and tunnel alignments; a pulse of light travels along each operating link in the direction of pumping. Statuses are as reported up to October 2026 and change often.

                Canals. Canals come from OpenStreetMap, which records no dig dates, so the project decides the era: “after 2014” = Kaleshwaram, Palamuru-Rangareddy and Sitarama works. The Kaleshwaram canal network table is an audited snapshot, not today's total; dug is not the same as lined and connected to fields.

                Kaleshwaram climb. The climb of about 500 m is the terrain height at Medigadda Barrage against Konda Pochamma Sagar, not the pumped head or the water levels.

                Sketch tool. Ground is read from the map's Terrain-DEM every 500 m, so a narrow ridge between samples can be missed. The verdict uses the proposal model's norms (canal fall, stage loss, tunnel and cut cover from proposal.json); it is a screening check, not a survey.

                Since 2014

                Telangana was formed in June 2014. The timeline shows dams, lifts and canals by the year they were done (rules under Timeline below).

                Tanks. OpenStreetMap records no construction or restoration date for tanks, and no source publishes which tanks Mission Kakatiya restored with their locations, so the before / after 2014 view is per district, not per tank. Mulugu and Narayanpet, carved out in 2019, are counted inside Jayashankar and Mahabubnagar.

                Change, not cause. Water projects are one reason; groundwater recovery, rainfall and the Mission Kakatiya tank restoration also contributed, so the map shows change, not cause.

                Groundwater & crops

                Groundwater. The state's 2023 resource assessment reports a rise of “more than 4 metres” between 2013 and 2023 against this network's deepest point in 2016 and 2019; the public well data stop in May 2023.

                Cropland. Share of each 3 km cell under crops (GLAD, Potapov et al. 2021), 2008–2011 against 2016–2019.

                Proposal

                A planning sketch, not a design: computed by propose_water.py from the 200 m terrain model, HydroRIVERS mean flows, ESA WorldCover land cover and the works already on the map, under norms and trunk systems set by a lift-irrigation engineering review. Surveys, real hydrology and inter-state awards would change every number.

                Everything drawn is new and marked “new”: a reservoir with its storage in TMC, a lift delivery (balancing tank) with TMC a year, a pump house with MW and lift, a canal or tunnel with km and TMC a year, and the land that gravity can serve from them. Solid trunk lines are new stages and dashed ones existing stages of the six multi-stage trunk systems. Each work takes water from the cheapest source with spare flow: a river intake, a reservoir or canal an existing scheme already fills, or a reservoir proposed here, so lifts cascade in stages as Kaleshwaram's do. The engineering caveats and the norms are listed under Details in the Proposal lens.

                3D models

                The 3D works are generic stand-ins, not as-built drawings. Dam bodies follow OpenStreetMap crest lines at the listed height (a 15 m stand-in in a lighter colour when the height is unknown); barrage piers every 20 m and the gates are schematic; where a barrage's listed height is at or above its ground level it is read as a water level and drawn as level − ground (at least 12 m); pump houses are generic buildings sized by MW, and several real ones are underground caverns; tunnels are drawn at the surface along their OSM line; reservoir surfaces sit at the terrain height along OSM's mapped shoreline, not a surveyed full level (proposal pools at the model's level); every height is raised by the same exaggeration as the terrain; works at approximate positions get no 3D model. The 3D models switch in Settings turns them off.

                Timeline

                Dams: opened, else began (construction start when the opening year is unknown). Links: done, else first water, construction start or planned, in that order. Canals: those dug after 2014 count as 2015 and older ones as 1970. Pump houses by the year they started pumping. Works without a year hide while the timeline is active. Play advances one year every 0.6 s; the 2014 mark jumps to the year Telangana was formed and Now shows everything. Tanks, the Mission Kakatiya shading and rivers are never filtered. In the Groundwater & crops lens with Depth or Cover, years before 2015 show the before-2014 window and later years the after window.
